## 2. In-Depth Dataset Analysis

### A. ASVspoof 2019 Logical Access (LA)
* **Provenance:** Official ASVspoof Consortium (University of Edinburgh, EURECOM, I2R).
* **Size:** ~121,400 audio files (Train: 25,380; Dev: 24,844; Eval: 71,237).
* **Labels:** `bonafide` vs `spoof` with attack algorithms (A01–A06 in train/dev; A07–A19 unseen in eval).
* **Strengths:** Established scientific standard for EER calculation; includes unseen algorithms in eval set.
* **Weaknesses:** Clean studio environment; single language (British English).
* **Relevance:** Essential baseline for evaluating zero-shot generalization across unseen TTS/VC algorithms.

### B. ASVspoof 2021 Deepfake (DF) Track
* **Provenance:** Official ASVspoof Consortium.
* **Size:** ~611,000 audio clips.
* **Labels:** `bonafide` vs `spoof` across varied lossy audio compression codecs.
* **Strengths:** Evaluates detection under severe channel and lossy compression degradation (MP3, AAC, OPUS, OGG at various bitrates).
* **Weaknesses:** Unbalanced distribution across codecs; large storage footprint.
* **Relevance:** Critical for verifying model robustness against real-world compressed audio streams.

### C. In-the-Wild Audio Dataset
* **Provenance:** Research team led by Müller et al. (CMU / Academic).
* **Size:** 31,431 audio files (19,963 bonafide, 11,468 spoof).
* **Labels:** Binary `bonafide` vs `spoof` collected from real-world media (YouTube, social media).
* **Strengths:** Contains actual deepfakes generated by modern commercial voice cloning engines (e.g., ElevenLabs, Bark, Tortoise).
* **Weaknesses:** Uncontrolled recording environments; variable audio durations.
* **Relevance:** Evaluates zero-shot performance on current commercial voice cloners.

### D. Google FLEURS (Indic Language Subsets)
* **Provenance:** Google Research.
* **Size:** ~12 hours per language across 102 languages.
* **Labels:** Native text transcriptions and speaker IDs for Hindi (`hi_in`), Tamil (`ta_in`), Telugu (`te_in`), Bengali (`bn_in`), Marathi (`mr_in`), Gujarati (`gu_in`), Malayalam (`ml_in`), Kannada (`kn_in`).
* **Strengths:** Native Indian accent and phonetic coverage for bonafide calibration.
* **Weaknesses:** Contains bonafide samples only; synthetic counterparts must be paired or generated under strict experimental protocol.
* **Relevance:** Mandatory baseline to ensure genuine Indian speakers are not misclassified as spoof.

## 3. Dataset Licensing & Ethical Acquisition Rules

1. **Explicit Licensing:** All datasets must have documented CC BY, MIT, or verified Academic Research licenses.
2. **No Proprietary / Private Data Theft:** Never ingest or commit non-consensual private audio recordings.
3. **Controlled Local Storage:** All raw datasets reside strictly in `data/raw/<dataset_id>/` and are ignored by Git (`.gitignore`).
